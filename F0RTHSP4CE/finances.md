# Finances

This page is the public high-level view of how F0 is funded.

It is not a private ledger or a resident operations manual. The goal is to explain the model clearly enough that people understand what keeps the space alive.

## What Costs Exist

F0 has ordinary recurring costs such as:

- rent;
- utilities;
- internet and basic infrastructure;
- consumables;
- occasional repairs, replacements, and cleaning;
- incremental space improvements.

## Where The Money Comes From

The main public funding sources are:

- resident contributions;
- member contributions;
- donations;
- occasional event-related donations or materials contributions.

F0 also benefits from non-cash support such as donated tools, materials, equipment, labor, maintenance, and documentation work.

## Current Contribution Levels

The current public baseline is:

- members: `$30` per month;
- residents: `$50` per month.

These numbers can change as the real situation of the space changes.

Current practical payment path for both member and resident fees: coordinate payment through `@Mike_Went`.

## What Those Contributions Support

The public high-level logic is:

- core recurring money goes to rent and utilities first;
- some money is needed as a safety cushion;
- some goes to shared consumables and maintenance;
- some can go toward room improvements, shared purchases, or infrastructure development.

## Budget Decisions

F0 is not run as a paid service business.

At a high level:

- small, reversible spending decisions can happen through ordinary do-ocracy and discussion;
- larger or more consequential spending should be discussed more openly;
- residents make the formal financial decisions when a formal decision is needed.

Money does not buy extra voting power, extra governance rights, or resident status.

## Donations

If you want to support F0 without becoming a member or resident, useful public options include:

- cash donations;
- donating tools or materials that are actually useful;
- supporting a specific event or practical need by agreement;
- contributing labor, maintenance, or documentation work.

F0 is not a commercial venue, and not every money-adjacent interaction should be framed as buying a service.

## What This Page Is Not

This page does not include:

- private payment details;
- private reimbursement details;
- internal debt tracking;
- internal access to finance tools.

Those belong in the private/internal documentation.
