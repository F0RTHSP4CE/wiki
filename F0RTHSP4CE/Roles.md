# Roles

F0 uses a small set of public-facing roles. This page is the quick comparison.

| Role | How you get it | Space access | Financial contribution | Responsibilities | Decision-making |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Guest | Visit a public event or come with a confirmed host | During events or while being hosted | None required | Follow public rules, respect people and property, do not assume unsupervised access | No formal voting role |
| Member | Become a trusted regular participant through the community process | Can access the hackspace for their own projects and everyday use | Pays a member fee | Use the space responsibly, contribute to community life, and do not treat membership as resident-level authority | Participates in discussion, but resident-only decisions stay with residents |
| Resident | Join through the residency process | Full everyday space access, plus resident-specific areas and responsibilities | Pays the main monthly contribution for rent, utilities, and development | Ongoing responsibility for the space, hosting, operations, and community continuity | Main formal decision-making role |

## Guest

Every person visiting the hackspace is a guest unless they are already a member or resident.

Guests usually come:

- to public events;
- for a hosted visit;
- to meet somebody or work on a clearly coordinated activity.

Guests can use shared resources when that is part of an event or when a member or resident explicitly says it is okay.

## Member

Members are trusted regular participants.

In practical terms, membership usually means:

- you are known to the community;
- you can access the space for your own projects without a resident needing to be present;
- you contribute financially at the member level;
- you are expected to behave like somebody helping the space function, not just consuming it;
- the resident room is not part of your default access without resident permission;
- you can host a guest only when at least one resident is already in the space;
- membership is the normal path before residency, not a shortcut around it.

For the joining path, see [Membership](membership.md).

## Resident

Residents are the people carrying the main long-term responsibility for F0.

That usually means:

- supporting rent, utilities, and development;
- taking part in meetings and formal decisions;
- helping with hosting, operations, and continuity;
- carrying access and trust that should not be treated casually.

For the joining path, see [Residency](residency.md).
