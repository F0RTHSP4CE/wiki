# Conflict Resolution

F0 is a horizontal community space. That means conflict will happen. The goal is not to pretend otherwise. The goal is to keep conflict from damaging people, events, and the space itself.

## Short Version

When something goes wrong:

1. try direct and respectful conversation if it is safe;
2. move the conflict out of the main channel / main room if it is escalating;
3. disengage if people are heated;
4. involve a neutral resident or mediator if needed;
5. if there is a safety issue, stop the immediate problem first.

## Start Direct, But Not Recklessly

Most ordinary misunderstandings are better handled early and directly:

- ask what happened;
- explain the impact on you;
- say what you want to change;
- keep it concrete.

Do not start with public shaming if a private conversation would reasonably solve it.

## If It Is Already Escalating

If a disagreement is turning into a public spiral:

- stop adding fuel in the main public space;
- move the discussion to a dedicated thread, direct messages, or an in-person conversation;
- take a break if people are no longer trying to understand each other.

Not every disagreement needs to stay visible to everybody forever.

## Use A Neutral Third Person

If direct conversation is not working, ask for help from someone neutral.

A useful mediator should:

- be trusted by both sides as much as possible;
- keep the discussion factual;
- prevent interruptions, insults, or topic drift;
- help define what would count as a workable next step.

## Safety Comes First

If somebody is being threatening, aggressively disruptive, or making the space feel unsafe:

- end the interaction for now;
- involve the host or a resident immediately;
- prioritize distance and de-escalation over finishing the debate.

In some cases that means asking somebody to leave for the day and discussing the deeper issue later.

## Formal Escalation

F0 also has internal processes for serious or repeated problems involving members and residents. Those procedures are intentionally documented in private/internal places, not here.

From the public side, the important point is this:

- direct conversation comes first when safe;
- mediation comes next when helpful;
- serious or repeated harm does get escalated.
