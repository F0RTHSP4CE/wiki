# Respect people

## Respect each person

- Do not insult, threaten, harass, humiliate, or corner a person.
- Stop the behavior when a person says that it is a problem.

## Stop when asked

- Stop the interaction when a person asks you to stop.
- Move a serious conflict out of the main room or public chat.

## Ask before recording

- Ask before you take a photograph of a person.
- Ask before you publish screens, notebooks, personal items, or projects.
