# Before leaving

This page is for the person who is closing up or might be the last person leaving the space.

In practice, this usually means a member or resident. Guests should not be left responsible for closing the space on their own.

## Turn Off

- soldering table power strip
- heating
- air conditioner
- main lights

## Leave On

- ventilation fan in the basement window, for air circulation

There is no need to re-check every decorative or auxiliary light, but it is normal to leave these on:

- LED matrix running Conway's Game of Life
- LED strip on shelves in openspace

## Check That It Is Closed

- back door
- entrance door

## Basic Cleanup

- throw away trash if bins are full
- replace bags if needed; new bags are in the top kitchen drawer
- clean up tables, especially in openspace
- do not leave dirty dishes or cups behind

Trash disposal map can be found on the exit door.

## Before You Actually Go

- check that nobody is being left in the space by mistake
- make sure any guest you brought has left with you or with their host
- if something is broken, unsafe, or unusual, report it in the appropriate chat instead of silently walking away

If you are unsure whether you are actually the person responsible for closing, clarify that before you leave.
