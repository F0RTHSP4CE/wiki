# Residency

We invite people to join residency when they are already part of the space in practice, not only in theory.

Residents are the people who:

- keep F0 running over time;
- make formal decisions together;
- support the space financially at the main level;
- take responsibility for hosting, operations, and continuity.

## What Changes After Joining

Residency usually means:

- full access to the space;
- a larger trust and responsibility footprint;
- participation in resident meetings and votes;
- access to resident-only areas and internal resources where appropriate.

Residency is not only "more access". It is a commitment to the community and the infrastructure behind it.

## Expected Path

The current practice is roughly:

1. visit and become a regular presence;
2. spend time in the space with real participation, not only remote interest;
3. become a member first;
4. spend at least about one month as a member;
5. ask to start the residency process and gather support from several residents;
6. meet residents, answer questions, and go through the community decision process.

Recent discussion strongly points to residency normally coming after at least about one month of membership, not as a first-step role. The recent working expectation has also been that a candidate arrives with support from three residents before the process moves forward.

In other words, membership is now the normal intermediate role: members are trusted to use the space on their own, but resident votes, resident-only areas, and resident hosting responsibility still stay at the resident level.

If you are not a member yet, read [Membership](membership.md) first.

## What Residents Are Expected To Do

Residency is a good fit if you want to:

- help keep the space alive, usable, and welcoming;
- host people and events from time to time;
- participate in discussions and decisions;
- contribute to projects, maintenance, documentation, or coordination;
- support the space financially on an ongoing basis.

Examples of contribution can include:

- maintaining infrastructure;
- organizing events;
- writing documentation;
- helping with space upkeep;
- running or supporting recurring community activity;
- taking ownership of a messy recurring problem and actually improving it.

## Meetings, Discussion, And Voting

Residents normally meet on Tuesday.

The usual flow is:

- bring the topic in advance;
- discuss it with enough context;
- interview or meet the candidate if needed;
- move to a resident vote when the discussion is mature enough.

## Financial Contribution

Residents support rent, utilities, and development.

The current public baseline is `$50` per month, while larger contributions are welcome. The exact burden can still change with the real situation of the space and the number of active residents.

Resident fees currently go through `@Mike_Went`.

## If You Are Interested

If residency sounds right for you:

- talk to residents in person;
- come to events and meetings;
- show what kind of participant you are in practice;
- then ask a resident to help start the process.
