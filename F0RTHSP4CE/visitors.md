# Visitors

F0RTHSP4CE is a community-run hackerspace in Tbilisi. The easiest ways to visit are:

- come to a public event;
- come to an announced open evening / open doors day;
- arrange a hosted visit in advance.

If you are new, start with the public [events page](https://f0rth.space/events.html), the [visit page](https://f0rth.space/visit-us.html), and the public chat topic for non-event visits: [Ask to visit](https://t.me/F0_PUBLIC_CHAT/29469).

## Who Is Welcome

You do not need to be a member or resident to visit.

Guests are welcome:

- at public events;
- when a resident agrees to host them;
- when a member agrees to host them and at least one resident is already in the space;
- for tours, conversations, project visits, and practical use of the space that fits the current activity in the space.

F0 is not a repair shop, a commercial coworking office, or a drop-in service desk. If you want help with a specific problem, write clearly what you are trying to do and do not assume somebody will fix it for you on the spot.

## Best Time To Come

The best first visit is usually a public event or open evening. Those are the times when:

- the space expects guests;
- there is usually a host;
- it is easier to get a tour and context.

If you want to come at another time, ask in advance and wait for confirmation from a host. For a first visit, that usually means a resident.

## How Non-Event Visits Work

For a non-event visit, write in [Ask to visit](https://t.me/F0_PUBLIC_CHAT/29469) with:

- the day and approximate time you want to come;
- how long you expect to stay;
- what you want to do;
- whether you need a tour, equipment access, or just a place to meet someone.

Please do not assume that "someone is inside" means the space is open for guests. A guest visit still needs a confirmed host. If a member is hosting you, at least one resident also needs to be in the space. If nobody confirms, treat that as "not today".

## What To Expect On A First Visit

On a good first visit, someone should:

- acknowledge that you are new;
- ask what brought you here;
- explain what is happening in the space today;
- offer a short tour if they have time;
- point out the basic guest rules.

F0 can be quiet, chaotic, social, workshop-like, or event-like depending on the day. Different people use the space differently. Sometimes you will get a full tour. Sometimes you will mostly be shown where to sit, who to ask, and what is happening.

## Guest Etiquette

- Ask before using tools, materials, or equipment.
- Ask before touching other people's projects, bags, laptops, or labeled items.
- Treat anything marked as private, resident-only, or owner-labeled as off-limits unless the owner says otherwise.
- Ask before taking photos. Be extra careful with personal projects, screens, notebooks, and people who may not want to appear in photos or videos.
- If you are unsure whether something is shared, ask.

## Public Areas, Tools, And Network

Guests can usually use public-facing areas and event infrastructure. Access to tools and equipment depends on the host, the event, and the current state of the space.

Guest Wi-Fi exists, but you should treat it as a public and untrusted network. Use the same caution you would use on public Wi-Fi anywhere else.

## Children, Animals, And Sensitive Visits

F0 is a workshop and event space, not a supervised child-oriented venue. If you want to come with children, animals, or a group that may need extra planning, ask in advance so a host can tell you whether that visit makes sense on that day.
